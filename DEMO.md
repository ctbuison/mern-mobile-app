# Shopfront demo script (8 minutes)

1. (0:00) One sentence: what Shopfront is and who it is for.
2. (0:30) Open the installed app. Show the About screen: live API address and update ID.
3. (1:00) Register a new account and sign in.
4. (1:45) Browse the products and open one.
5. (2:30) Add two items to the cart. Show the badge.
6. (3:15) Use one native feature: take a photo or show the location.
7. (4:00) Close the app completely and reopen it. Still signed in.
8. (4:30) Architecture slide: app, Render API, Atlas. Say one trade-off.
9. (6:30) What I would build next.
10. (7:30) Questions.
